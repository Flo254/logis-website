Your Strategic Supply Chain Partner in Kenya

Founded with a vision to simplify freight forwarding and cargo transport in East Africa, Logis Kenya has grown into one of the country's most respected logistics providers. Headquartered along Mombasa Road in Nairobi with branch facilities at the Port of Mombasa, we provide an unbreakable link between marine shipping, rail transport, and road distribution.

  • Comprehensive Inter-County Network

    Scheduled daily departures connecting Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, and border transit corridors.

  • Stringent Safety & Regulatory Compliance

    Fully compliant with NTSA safety standards, KRA customs clearance guidelines, and KEBS cargo protection protocols.

  • Real-Time GPS Tracking & Transparency

    Every shipment is tracked 24/7 with instant milestone alerts, leaving no guesswork about your delivery status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our standards, operating model, and cargo handling procedures

Where are your primary warehouse facilities located in Kenya?

Our main distribution and bonded warehousing hubs are situated on Mombasa Road near the Inland Container Depot (ICD) in Nairobi, and along the airport corridor in Changamwe, Mombasa. We also operate secondary transit depots in Nakuru, Eldoret, and Kisumu.

Are you licensed to handle Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) bonded cargo?

Yes. Logis Kenya holds valid KRA customs agent licenses, Kentrade accreditation, and transit bond permits to clear and transport bonded cargo directly from Mombasa Port to any designated dry port or bonded warehouse in East Africa.

What measures are taken to guarantee shipment security?

All trucks feature tamper-proof electronic cargo seals, dual GPS tracking units with geofencing alerts, continuous video telematics, and armed escort arrangements available for high-value consignments.

How can a new enterprise client open a monthly account?

Businesses can submit an inquiry through our contact or quote forms, or email us at info@logis.co.ke. We verify your company registration details and issue credit terms within 48 hours.
