Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding freight, clearance, and deliveries in Kenya

How fast can you deliver parcels within Nairobi and countrywide?

Deliveries within Nairobi and its environs (Kiambu, Machakos, Kajiado) are completed on the same day. Deliveries to all other counties take between 24 and 48 hours depending on destination.

How does the shipment tracking work?

Once your consignment is booked and loaded, you receive a unique tracking code via SMS and email. You can check the live GPS location of your truck or parcel anytime from your phone or computer.

Can you clear imported cargo directly at the Port of Mombasa?

Yes. Our certified KRA customs clearance team operates inside Kilindini Port, facilitating verification, duties settlement, Kentrade approvals, and immediate container evacuation via road or SGR rail.

What payment methods can I use?

We support M-Pesa (Till/Paybill), Bank Transfers (EFT/RTGS), major credit/debit cards, and corporate credit accounts for regular commercial shippers.

Is my freight insured during transit?

Every shipment is covered under our comprehensive Goods-In-Transit (GIT) policy, protecting your goods against accidental loss, damage, or road hazards.
