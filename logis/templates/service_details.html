Reliable Inter-County Road Freight & Port-to-Door Transportation

Logis Kenya operates a modern, GPS-monitored fleet of trucks, flatbeds, and box trailers capable of transporting dry cargo, reefer containers, and oversized industrial equipment. With strategic dispatch depots in Mombasa, Nairobi, Nakuru, Eldoret, and Kisumu, we guarantee seamless scheduling and prompt arrivals.

  • Direct clearance and evacuation of containers from the Port of Mombasa and Nairobi ICD.
  • Real-time satellite GPS tracking with automated SMS and email milestone notifications.
  • Comprehensive marine and inland goods-in-transit (GIT) insurance coverage on every consignment.

Whether you require consolidated less-than-truckload (LTL) deliveries or dedicated full-truckload (FTL) charters, our drivers and logistics officers follow strict safety compliance and defensive driving standards to ensure your cargo arrives in pristine condition.

Our customer support desk is available around the clock to assist you with customs documentation, weighbridge clearances, SGR coordination, and express route planning across Kenya and border crossings to Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda.
