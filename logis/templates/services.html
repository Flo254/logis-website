Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Kenyan and regional transport services

How fast can you transport cargo between Mombasa and Nairobi?

Standard road freight between the Port of Mombasa and Nairobi takes between 18 to 24 hours under normal transit conditions. We also coordinate express Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) rail freight with same-day transfers to the Nairobi Inland Container Depot (ICD).

Do you provide real-time GPS tracking for every shipment?

Yes. Every vehicle in our fleet is integrated with satellite GPS tracking. Clients receive an automated tracking link with live map updates and automated milestone SMS notifications as your shipment passes each checkpoint.

Is my cargo insured while in transit?

All consignments handled by Logis Kenya are covered by our comprehensive Goods-In-Transit (GIT) policy. For specialized or exceptionally high-value shipments, we also offer extended all-risks marine and inland cargo insurance.

What payment methods do you accept in Kenya?

We accept M-Pesa (Buy Goods Till / Paybill), direct bank wire transfers (RTGS/EFT), major credit/debit cards, and 30-day invoiced corporate credit accounts for vetted enterprise partners.

Do you handle cross-border transport to neighboring countries?

Yes. We operate cross-border routes along the Northern and Central transport corridors serving Uganda (Kampala, Jinja), Tanzania (Dar es Salaam, Arusha), Rwanda (Kigali), and South Sudan (Juba) with complete COMESA customs bond coverage.
